Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

How do I apply for a course?

You can apply online through our website, visit our campus, or call our admissions team. The application process typically takes 24-48 hours, and we'll guide you through every step including course selection and financial planning.

What are your admission requirements?

Admission requirements vary by program. Generally, we require a high school diploma or equivalent, and some programs may have specific prerequisites. Our admissions team will assess your background and recommend the best starting point for your goals.

Do you offer financial aid or payment plans?

Yes, we offer various financial aid options including scholarships, payment plans, and partnerships with employers. Our financial aid advisors can help you explore all available options to make your education affordable.

Can I schedule a campus tour?

Absolutely! We offer both in-person and virtual campus tours. You can schedule a tour through our website, by phone, or by visiting our campus. Tours are available Monday through Saturday and include meetings with instructors and current students.

What support services do you provide to students?

We provide comprehensive support including academic tutoring, career counseling, job placement assistance, technical support, and mental health resources. Our student success team is dedicated to helping you achieve your goals throughout your learning journey.
